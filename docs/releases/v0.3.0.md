Kitden 0.3.0 is a new page with a new design. The Gateway and the Library
are maps you can drag on, every change waits in one preview tray, and the Kitden gateway runs
on your Mac with tool rules per project.

## New

- **Preview tray.** A button, a switch, a link's × or a drag only adds a row to one tray at the
  bottom left. Apply all writes every row as one change, all or nothing, with one backup; Undo
  all takes it all back. "Show changes" opens a row's exact file change.
- **Kitden gateway.** A gateway on your Mac, with no deploy and no account. Move servers into it,
  local or remote, and every client that uses it reaches them. Turn a whole server or one tool
  on or off for all projects or for one project; a project's folders and worktrees follow it. A
  tool you turn off is refused at once, also in open sessions.
- **Gateway map.** Clients, the gateway, its servers and their tools in one tree. Drag a server
  into the gateway, a server onto a client to move it out, or a client onto the gateway to
  connect it. Point at a link and press × to disconnect, take out or turn off. A List sits one
  click away.
- **Strict mode.** A server offers only the tools that are on; a new tool waits until you
  decide. Each tool says whether it reads or may write, and how often it was called.
- **Add a server, a plugin or a skill** from the marketplaces on this computer, by a click or a
  drag onto the map.
- **Library.** One page for plugins, skills, hooks and instructions, as a map or a List with a
  column per client. Drag a plugin or a skill onto a client to add it; a link's × removes it or
  turns it off. Differences shows what differs between your clients, and "Add missing plugins
  and skills…" fills the gaps.
- **Instructions.** See CLAUDE.md, AGENTS.md, Cursor rules, Claude Code's commands and agents, and
  what CLAUDE.md pulls in. Kitden only reads them.
- **More ways to manage.** Remove a plugin or a skill, add a server, skill or plugin to another
  client or into one project, disconnect a client from Kitden, and move a server out of the
  gateway into one client. Each has Undo.
- **Overview.** "Your setup, connected.": one-button cards for what needs you and what can
  improve, and a small map of your setup.
- **Changes.** Every change by day, with its history, Undo, "Redo…" and "See it on the map".
- **Secrets in the Keychain.** A moved server's secrets go to your macOS Keychain, and a backup
  or a change record says when it holds a secret.
- **`kitden demo`.** Try the page on a sample setup, with nothing on your computer read or
  changed.
- **New look.** The mockup's colours, Inter and JetBrains Mono shipped with Kitden, line icons, a
  new rail and top bar, and calm motion that shows what changed. The logo's door glows and a
  little smoke drifts from the d when the page opens.

## Better

- The page has its data in under a second. Codex's slow plugin list and the client versions
  come in the background, and Rescan reads the versions again.
- Details say in words where each client loads an item and what a change reaches. Paths and
  commands wait under Technical details.
- A review leads with what will happen and where, and promises only the Undo it can keep.
- "Unknown" means Kitden could not read something, never that it is missing. An item from a file
  that breaks keeps its last good state, marked as such.
- "Needs review" marks a same-name item from another source, so Kitden never copies only one
  side.
- The address keeps the project and the item you opened, so a link or Back returns to it.
- Kitden reads Claude Code's local project settings, also at a git repository's root.
- Kitden writes only in your home folder and your known projects.
- A map draw lands even when the browser holds frames back.

## Fixed

- Undo and a failed Apply also run the client's own commands for a mixed change, and say what
  could not be put back.
- A marketplace source that holds a password or a token is refused.
